# Tenda — Capital & Partnership Ledger

_Funding, grants, fellowships and partnerships mapped to Tenda's product stage. Compiled August 2026 against Tenda v0.4.3-testnet (audit pending)._

**Interactive version:** https://claude.ai/code/artifact/0ab3ce43-658c-4c98-a326-6435da2cc772

Every row is filed under the **condition that releases it** — the same logic as Tenda's own escrow. Figures marked `[Confirmed Aug 2026]` were checked against public sources this month; `[verify]` rows reflect general programme knowledge and need confirming before use. The **Apply at** column links the page that actually accepts an application where one exists — not every programme has an open form, and those are labelled as such.

**64 opportunities across 9 categories** — 35 actionable now, 25 gated on audit or scale, 4 not worth chasing this cycle.

## The constraint

| Stage | Status | What it unlocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Testnet, open source, no users** | Where Tenda is today | Audit subsidies · hackathons & bounties · ecosystem grants for public goods · pilot-stage impact capital · distribution partnerships |
| **Gate 1 — third-party audit complete** | Prerequisite, not an option | Mainnet deployment · bug bounties · fiat off-ramp partnerships · any consumer press or growth spend |

## Fellowships for the core team

They are slow, personal and competitive, and they should run as a background process rather than a quarter's focus. The 2026 Echoing Green cycle has already closed — note the September pattern and prepare rather than scramble.

## Argues in your favour

- Three chains, three independent grant pools. Most applicants can approach one ecosystem. Tenda has legitimate standing with Solana, Base and Celo simultaneously, for the same work.
- The gas problem is actually solved, not promised. USDC paying its own gas on Celo and a covered first escrow on Solana are shipped, demonstrable answers to the objection every consumer-crypto funder raises.
- Open source with test suites, on both VMs. This is the qualifying condition for most public-goods funding, and Tenda already meets it.
- Real markets, named specifically. Lagos, Nairobi, Accra, Johannesburg, plus M-Pesa, MoMo and GCash settlement. Development finance funds this profile; abstract global gig platforms do not get funded.
- The honesty is an asset. A team that publishes "auto-approve is designed and not yet shipped" is a team whose numbers a reviewer will believe. Lead with that posture rather than hiding it.

## Will be raised against you

- No audit. Nothing consumer-facing gets funded past this. It is the whole plan's critical path.
- No users and no transactions. Retroactive and threshold-gated programmes — Base Builder Grants, the Celo Anchor pool, Optimism retro funding — are simply unreachable until this changes.
- Mediation is centralised. "Tenda mediation instructs the contract within 24h" is a trusted human step inside a product whose pitch is that you should not have to trust Tenda. Expect this question in every technical review.
- Auto-approve is not shipped. A silent poster strands a worker's funds. This is the flaw a careful reviewer will find on their own if you do not name it first.
- The Exchange surface is a regulated activity. Peer-to-peer fiat trading across eight currencies plausibly requires VASP or money-transmission registration in most target markets. No funder will ignore this, and it is currently unaddressed in public materials.
- Two open questions on your own fact sheet. Founder and mainnet date are both listed as unknown. Funders do not fund anonymous teams with undated roadmaps.

## Three fixes worth more than any single application

- **Name the founder and date the mainnet launch** — Both sit as "Unknown" on Tenda's own fact sheet. Every grant application, accelerator form and fellowship requires both. This is an afternoon of decisions that unblocks the entire page.
- **Write the licensing position down, even if it is provisional** — A one-page statement of how the Exchange surface is structured, which markets are live, and what advice has been taken. Not having an answer is far worse than having a conservative one.
