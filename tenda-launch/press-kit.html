01 · The statement

What Tenda is

The canonical description. Please quote from this rather than paraphrasing.

Canonical · 100 words

Tenda is a rewarding onchain microtasking application.

Post a task from anywhere. Take a task from anywhere. The moment your completed task is accepted, you are paid — not queued for payout, not scheduled for Friday, not held for thirty days.

Tenda is not a marketplace. A marketplace’s job ends when two people find each other. Tenda’s job ends when the work is done and the money has moved. Posting, assignment, completion and settlement all happen in one app, onchain, so that paid is a state of the task rather than a promise about next month.

Small work. Real money. No gap.

02 · The mechanic

One loop. Five states. No gap.

A task on Tenda has five states. On other platforms the fifth is a separate process with its own queue, threshold and calendar. Here it carries the same timestamp as the fourth.

 StateTimeWhat happens
01POST14:01:12A task is created onchain, from anywhere
02ASSIGN14:01:20The task is assigned to the person who will do it
03DO14:02:03The work is completed and submitted in the app
04ACCEPT14:02:07The submission is accepted
05PAID14:02:07Settlement happens onchain

States 04 and 05 share a timestamp

That simultaneity is the entire product. It is possible because the task and the payment live on the same system — which is also why Tenda is an application rather than a marketplace.

You cannot make payment part of finishing a task if the payment runs on a different system than the task.

03 · The problem

The gap between done and paid

Nobody quits a microtasking app because the tasks were too hard. They quit at the payout screen — the minimum, the hold, the country list. The category’s reputation problem lives entirely in one interval.

Typical microtask platformup to 30 days · minimum threshold · country list
Work donePaid
Tenda0 · no threshold · no country check
Work donePaid

A thirty-day hold is not a delay. It is a loan. The work happened in week one and the money moves in week five, and in between somebody is holding it — and it isn’t the person who did the work.

06 · For writers

A note on how to describe Tenda

Tenda is an application, not a marketplace. This is a description of the product rather than a branding preference, and getting it wrong changes what the story says — it is precisely the distinction that makes settlement at acceptance possible.

Instead ofPlease write
Tenda, a marketplace for gig workTenda, an onchain microtasking app
a platform connecting workers with employersan app where tasks are posted, completed and paid
users list their servicesposters create tasks; earners complete them
a crypto gig marketplacean onchain microtasking application
workers request a payoutearners are paid when a task is accepted
the Tenda ecosystemthe Tenda app

Terms we use

Poster creates a task. Earner completes it. A task is the unit of work. The loop is post → assign → do → accept → paid. Settlement is the payment event.

09 · Before this ships

Status of this kit

Draft — not yet for distribution

Every claim in this kit derives from Tenda’s own product statements. The fields marked Pending could not be verified and have deliberately been left blank rather than estimated — a press kit is copied verbatim by every outlet that quotes it, so a wrong number travels much faster than a correction.

Two answers matter more than the rest. The measured settlement time, which should replace the word “instant” everywhere it appears — “paid in under four seconds” persuades where “paid instantly” does not, because it reads as a measurement. And three real example tasks with real amounts: this kit currently describes small paid work without naming a single instance of it, and abstraction is the enemy of a category this heavily doubted.
