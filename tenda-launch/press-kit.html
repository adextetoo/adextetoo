01 · The statement

What Tenda is

The canonical description. Please quote from this rather than paraphrasing.

Canonical · 100 words

Tenda is an on-chain escrow application for small paid work.

The money locks in a contract the moment a gig posts — USDC, SOL or ETH, on Solana, Base or Celo. The worker delivers and uploads photo proof. The poster approves with one signature, and in that same transaction the contract splits the funds: 97.5% to the worker, 2.5% to Tenda. Seconds, not days.

Nobody holds your money in between — not the counterparty, not an exchange, not Tenda. There is no admin key, no pause button, no sweep function.

Getting paid isn’t a promise. It’s a release.

Short · 25 words

Tenda is an on-chain escrow app for small paid work. Funds lock when a gig posts, proof releases them, and settlement takes seconds. Nobody holds your money.

02 · The mechanic

Lock. Work. Approve. Release.

The money is locked before the work begins — that is the whole product. When the worker is paid, the funds don’t travel; approval opens a lock that was already closed around them.

01 · POSTER

Lock

Funds leave the poster’s wallet and enter the escrow contract. No longer in either party’s control.

< 2s · on-chain
02 · WORKER

Work

The worker accepts and delivers. Photo or video proof is uploaded in the app.

proof submitted
03 · POSTER

Approve

The poster reviews the proof and signs. One tap. No back-office, no phone calls.

one signature
04 · PROGRAM · ATOMIC

Release

The same transaction splits the locked funds — payout to the worker, fee to Tenda.

settles in seconds
Worked example 12.00 USDC locked → 11.70 worker + 0.30 fee · 2.5%

Tenda holds nothing at any stage. No admin key, no pause button, no sweep function. Every state change is a transaction verifiable in the block explorer of the chain it settled on.

03 · When it goes wrong

Funds are never stuck

Every gig has a deterministic exit — by a party, by a deadline, or by mediation. Whichever triggers, both sides get the same on-chain receipt.

RouteTriggerOutcomeStatus
CancelPoster cancels before a worker acceptsFunds return to the lockerLive
ReclaimWorker misses the proof deadlinePoster claims the refundLive
DisputeEither side escalates after proofTenda mediation instructs the contract, within 24hLive
Auto-approvePoster neither approves nor disputes in 48hFunds auto-release to the workerPlanned

Auto-approve is designed and not yet shipped. Until it is, a poster who goes silent leaves funds locked until someone opens a dispute. We would rather state that than have it discovered.

04 · Onboarding

The hardest part of crypto, removed

Most people quit at “first, buy a gas token.” Tenda deletes that step on every chain it ships on — each one a different rail, all of them invisible to the user.

Solana Live

  • Native escrow program, written in Anchor
  • One-time SOL grant covers a new user’s first full escrow
  • Reduced platform fee on Solana Mobile (Seeker)
  • Phantom, Solflare + wallet-adapter support

Base Partial

Coinbase’s L2 — USDC-native, built to onboard everyone.

  • TendaEscrow Solidity contracts on testnet
  • Gasless USDC approvals via EIP-2612 permit

Celo Live

Mobile-first L2 where stablecoins pay their own gas.

  • Gas paid in USDC via feeCurrency — no CELO required
  • Same contracts, same USDC, zero extra tokens to hold
  • cUSD supported on the exchange

Wallets: Phantom, Solflare, and 400+ others via Reown AppKit — or start with none at all.

09 · Before this ships

Three things to fix first

01

Change the site label “Marketplace · Gigs” to “Gigs”

The positioning says Tenda is an application, not a marketplace. The site says otherwise in the one place every journalist screenshots. One word, and no media-kit language outweighs the product arguing against itself.

02

Launch in two tracks — builders now, consumers at mainnet

Pitching consumer tech press a “get paid instantly” earning app that is unaudited testnet invites a correction cycle that never fully clears. The pre-audit, open-source story is genuinely strong with crypto and developer press, and costs nothing to run now.

03

Stop saying “paid instantly after completing a task”

The contract pays in seconds on approval, not on completion — and auto-approve is planned, not live. The accurate line is stronger anyway: the money was locked before the worker started, and they can verify it in a block explorer.
