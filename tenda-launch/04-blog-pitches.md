# Tenda — Tech Blog Pitch Pack

Ten outlet-tiers, ten different stories. **The same product, never the same pitch.** A journalist who receives the identical email their competitor received files nothing.

### Rules for this pack

1. **One angle per outlet.** Track which quote and which angle each outlet gets; never double-assign.
2. **Subject lines under 60 characters.** They are read in a preview pane on a phone.
3. **No attachments.** One press-kit link. Attachments trip spam filters and nobody opens a 4MB PDF on deadline.
4. **The first sentence is the whole pitch.** If the story isn't clear by the end of line one, the rest doesn't get read.
5. **Never send to a general tips inbox and a named reporter simultaneously.** Pick the person.
6. **Follow up once. Once.** Then move on and stay friendly — the second launch is easier than the first only if you were pleasant during the first.
7. **Offer the screen recording in every pitch.** The unedited post-to-paid clip converts scepticism faster than any paragraph.
