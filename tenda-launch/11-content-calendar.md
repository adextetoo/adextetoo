# Tenda — Content Calendar

Day-by-day, from T-21 to T+30. **T = launch day.**

Dependencies matter more than dates here. The single most common way a launch like this fails is publishing the press release before the proof video exists — journalists then write from the claim instead of the evidence, and the evidence is the only thing that distinguishes Tenda from a category full of identical promises.

## Ordering rules

1. **Proof before claims.** No press asset ships before Script E exists. The video is the campaign's foundation, not its decoration.
2. **Tier 3 before Tier 1.** The emerging-markets angle is the strongest story and the least contested inbox. Coverage there makes the Tier-1 pitch warmer.
3. **Nothing publishes with a bracket in it.** T-21 gates the entire calendar.
4. **Founder replies beat founder posts.** On launch day, an hour of honest replies in a sceptical thread is worth more than any scheduled content.
5. **The transactional emails matter more than the campaign emails.** "Paid. $4.20." will be opened more than every message in the sequence combined.

---

## What would make this calendar wrong

Worth stating up front, so it's recognised early rather than argued about later:

- **If the app is not genuinely ready**, every date moves. A launch that produces coverage and a broken first task is worse than no launch — in this category, the first wave of "it didn't pay me" posts is very hard to outrun.
- **If settlement is slower than "instant" implies**, the entire campaign angle needs rewriting before anything ships, not after. Change the words, not the evidence.
- **If the acceptance and verification model isn't solid**, expect the first critical piece within a week of launch, and prepare the honest answer now rather than in a hurry.
