## Ordering rules

1. **The site label changes before any pitch goes out.** It is one word and it contradicts the whole positioning.
2. **The explorer clip exists before any press asset ships.** It is the campaign's foundation.
3. **The strongest story goes to emerging-markets press first** — it converts better than general tech and makes later pitches warmer.
4. **Testnet status appears in every asset, every time.** No exceptions for length.
5. **Answer the hostile questions first**, on every channel, on every day.

---

## What would change this plan

- **If the audit finds something material**, everything stops and the fix becomes the story. Publishing what was found and fixed is worth more than the original launch.
- **If auto-approve slips**, Track B slips with it. Don't ship a consumer promise the contract can't keep.
- **If mainnet lands before the audit publishes**, do not run Track B anyway. The audit is the gate, not the network.
